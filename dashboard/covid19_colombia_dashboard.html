Case overview

Three takeaways for presenting the dashboard
Finding 1

The epidemic trajectory was nonlinear

The series contains several epidemic waves. The SIR model is therefore useful as an interpretive framework, but not as the strongest final predictive model.

Finding 2

The final model captured recent dynamics

Lag-based regression achieved strong fit and low error in the test window, supporting its selection as the final predictive model.

Finding 3

Epidemiological pressure occurred in waves

Monthly and daily peaks reveal periods of concentrated transmission followed by declines and relative stabilization.
